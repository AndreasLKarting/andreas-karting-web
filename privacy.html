Privacy statement

Last updated: 20 October 2026

This website (alracing.nl) presents the karting career of Andreas Lazaridis. We keep data collection to a minimum: there are no accounts, no forms, no advertising and no selling of data.

Who is responsible

Andreas is a minor, so the website, its social media and its communication are run by his parents on his behalf. For any privacy question, write to info@alracing.nl.

What we publish about Andreas

The site shows his name, photos, videos, race results and sporting achievements to promote his racing and to find sponsors. His parents have agreed to this. We publish his year of birth and the province he lives in (South Holland), but not his address, school, full date of birth or personal contact details, and we do not name other minors.

What we collect about visitors

1. Website statistics, only with your permission

If you click Accept analytics, we load Google Analytics 4. It measures page views, the pages you visit, clicks on our YouTube and Instagram links, your approximate region, and your device, browser and language. It sets cookies (_ga and _ga_…) with a random identifier. We use this only to understand how the site is used and to improve it. Data is kept for at most 14 months.

Legal basis: your consent. If you click Reject analytics nothing is loaded. You can change your choice at any time with cookie settings or by clearing the cookies for this site in your browser.

2. Hosting and delivery

The site is hosted on GitHub Pages and delivered through Cloudflare. These services process your IP address and basic request details to deliver the site, keep it secure and measure overall performance. Cloudflare Web Analytics does not use cookies. Legal basis: our legitimate interest in running a secure website.

3. Content from other services

  • Google Fonts: fonts are loaded from Google servers, which receive your IP address.
  • YouTube: video thumbnails load from YouTube servers. A video is only played, in YouTube’s privacy-enhanced mode, after you click it.
  • Google Sheets: the race calendar, social numbers, photos, videos and the page texts are read from a published Google Sheet that we maintain. It contains no visitor data.

Legal basis: our legitimate interest in showing the site as designed. These providers have their own privacy policies.

4. Your browser

The website stores small items in your browser (local storage): your analytics choice, your language choice and a copy of the page texts, so the page loads faster. They are not sent to us.

5. If you email us

If you contact us, for example about sponsorship, we use your email address and message only to reply, and keep them as long as needed for that conversation.

Sharing and transfers

We do not sell data or share it for advertising. The services above may process data outside the EU, based on the safeguards those companies provide (for example the EU–US Data Privacy Framework).

Your rights

You can ask us for access to, correction or deletion of your data, restrict or object to its use, ask for a copy, and withdraw consent at any time. Email us and we will respond within one month. You also have the right to complain to the Dutch Data Protection Authority, the Autoriteit Persoonsgegevens.

Changes

If we change what we collect, we will update this page and the date above.
