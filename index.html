01 — Profile

Who is Andreas?

Andreas started karting in 2023 with KWM Racing and since 2026 trains and races with Den Hartog Racing.

In 2026 he competes in the NK Huurkarten (Junior/Mini), FK Huurkarten (Light/Pupilen) and the Dutch Racing Series - DRS (Junior), Sodi World Series - SWS and Dutch Outdoor Karting Championship (DOKC) with podiums at Circuit Park Berghem, Euro Indoor Karting Swalmen and the SWS Series in Thessaloniki.

His goal is to fight at the front every weekend and keep improving his race craft. He films his onboard sessions and shares them with a growing audience of racing fans.
